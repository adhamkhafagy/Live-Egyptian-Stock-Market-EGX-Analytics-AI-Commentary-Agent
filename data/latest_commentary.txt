### English
The session was mostly down, with more stocks losing ground than gaining. The biggest winners were Oriental Weavers, up 7.11% to EGP 25.00, and Alexandria Mineral Oils, up 2.21% to EGP 14.31. The sharpest declines came from Palm Hills Development, down 7.02% to EGP 10.60, and Abou Kir Fertilizers, down 3.95% to EGP 84.55. Orascom Construction was unchanged at EGP 71.05, while several other names slipped between 1% and 2% lower. Overall, the index closed with a modest negative bias.
