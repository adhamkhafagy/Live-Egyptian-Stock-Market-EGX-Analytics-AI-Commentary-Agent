### English
The EGX closed with a mixed session, as gains were offset by a handful of declines. Misr Fertilizers Production led the market with a 9.03% rise to EGP 46.50, followed by Abou Kir Fertilizers up 6.82% at EGP 94.00. On the downside, Palm Hills Development fell the most, down 2.11% to EGP 14.40, while Eastern Company slipped 1.13% to EGP 35.76. Broadly, the index saw modest advances from sectors such as housing and chemicals, with Heliopolis Housing up 4.38% and Alexandria Mineral Oils up 1.89%. The remaining blue‑chip stocks traded flat to slightly lower, keeping the overall market movement limited.
