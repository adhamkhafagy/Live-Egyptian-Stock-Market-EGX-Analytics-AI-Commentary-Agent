### English
Today's EGX session was mixed with a slight upside as a handful of stocks posted modest gains while most remained unchanged. The biggest risers were EFG Holding, up 2.02% to EGP 26.20, and e‑Finance Investment Group, up 1.98% to EGP 25.29. On the downside, Alexandria Mineral Oils fell 2.08% to EGP 12.25 and Talaat Moustafa Group slipped 1.81% to EGP 78.06. Other notable moves included Telecom Egypt gaining 0.73% and Commercial International Bank losing 0.02%. Overall, the index ended largely flat.
