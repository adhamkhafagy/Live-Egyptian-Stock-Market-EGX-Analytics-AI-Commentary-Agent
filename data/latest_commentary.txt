### English
Today's EGX session was mostly up, with gains outnumbering losses. The biggest riser was Talaat Moustafa Group, which jumped 6.03% to EGP 89.90, followed by Eastern Company gaining 4.53% to EGP 30.00. On the downside, Oriental Weavers fell 2.39% to EGP 25.76, and e‑Finance Investment Group slipped 1.24% to EGP 23.90. Other notable gainers included Misr Fertilizers Production (+2.54%) and Commercial International Bank (+2.46%). The market closed with a mix of modest advances and declines across sectors.
