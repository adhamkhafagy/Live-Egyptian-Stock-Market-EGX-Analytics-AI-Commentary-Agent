### English
The session was mostly up, with the majority of listed stocks posting gains. e‑Finance Investment Group led the rally, rising 5.11% to EGP 24.70, while Telecom Egypt added 3.32% to close at EGP 134.00. On the downside, Abou Kir Fertilizers fell 2.69% to EGP 74.25 and Misr Fertilizers Production slipped 2.59% to EGP 37.60. Other notable gainers included Eastern Company (+2.20%), Oriental Weavers (+2.12%) and Elsewedy Electric (+2.09%). Overall, price movements were modest, with most active stocks changing between 0.5% and 2%.
