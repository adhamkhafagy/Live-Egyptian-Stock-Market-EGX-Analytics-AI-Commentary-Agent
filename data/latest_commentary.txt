### English
Today's EGX session was mostly up, with gains outnumbering losses across the board. Palm Hills Development led the rally, rising 4.71% to EGP 11.55, while Credit Agricole Egypt added 3.75% to close at EGP 23.25. The only stock in the red was Oriental Weavers, down 0.52% to EGP 24.70, followed by Alexandria Mineral Oils, which inched up just 0.08% to EGP 12.51. Broadly, most indices reflected modest advances, and the market closed on a positive note.
