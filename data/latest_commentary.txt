### English
The session was mixed, with gains offset by comparable losses across the EGX. The biggest riser was Abou Kir Fertilizers, up 4.00% to EGP 90.00, followed by e‑Finance Investment Group, which climbed 1.77% to EGP 25.23. The steepest declines came from Eastern Company, down 2.72% to EGP 28.21, and Elsewedy Electric, which slipped 1.69% to EGP 116.00. Several other stocks moved modestly, including a flat close for Orascom Construction and small gains for Heliopolis Housing and Oriental Weavers. Overall, the market displayed balanced momentum with no clear directional bias.
