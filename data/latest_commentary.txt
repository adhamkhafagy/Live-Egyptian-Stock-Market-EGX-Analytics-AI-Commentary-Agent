### English
The session was mostly down, with the majority of stocks losing value. EFG Holding and Orascom Construction were the only stocks that held steady, each unchanged at 0.00%. Heliopolis Housing fell the most, down 6.21%, followed by e‑Finance Investment Group, down 5.60%. Commercial International Bank slipped 1.95% and Credit Agricole Egypt fell 2.69%. Overall, the market showed a clear bearish bias as most sectors posted losses.
