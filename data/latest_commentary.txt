### English
The session was mostly up, with the majority of listed stocks posting modest gains. e‑Finance Investment Group led the rally, rising 2.12% to EGP 25.53, followed by Palm Hills Development up 1.04% at EGP 11.62. On the downside, Telecom Egypt fell the most, dropping 3.47% to EGP 137.02, while Alexandria Mineral Oils slipped 0.90% to EGP 13.14. Other notable moves included modest advances in EFG Holding (+0.79%) and Abou Kir Fertilizers (+0.20%). Overall market volatility was limited and the broader direction remained positive.
