### English
The EGX session was mostly down, with the majority of listed stocks slipping. The biggest gainers were Elsewedy Electric, up 1.54% to EGP 117.90, and Telecom Egypt, rising 0.89% to EGP 129.70. The sharpest declines were seen in Misr Fertilizers Production, down 5.85% to EGP 38.60, and Abou Kir Fertilizers, down 5.78% to EGP 76.30. Other notable losers included Eastern Company (‑2.01%), Credit Agricole Egypt (‑1.88%) and EFG Holding (‑1.18%). Meanwhile, a few stocks such as e‑Finance Investment Group and Orascom Construction held steady, reflecting modest negative pressure on the market.
