### English
The session was mostly down, with the market pressured by several sizable losses. Elsewedy Electric led the gainers, up **1.39%** to **EGP 103.44**, followed by Heliopolis Housing, which rose **1.31%** to **EGP 6.20**. The biggest drags came from Telecom Egypt, down **5.69%** at **EGP 117.00**, and Misr Fertilizers Production, down **5.51%** to **EGP 44.60**. Other notable declines included Palm Hills Development (‑3.44%) and Commercial International Bank (‑1.58%). Gains were limited to a handful of stocks while most of the market slipped.
