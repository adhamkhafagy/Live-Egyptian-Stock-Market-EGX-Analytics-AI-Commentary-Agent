### English
Today's EGX session was mostly up, with the majority of listed stocks posting gains. The biggest rally came from EFG Holding, which jumped 6.75% to EGP 25.61, followed by Talaat Moustafa Group gaining 2.99% to EGP 86.00. Among the laggards, Alexandria Mineral Oils slipped 1.59% to EGP 13.01, while Orascom Construction was flat at EGP 71.05. Gains were also seen in Oriental Weavers (up 2.86%), Commercial International Bank (up 2.30%) and Telecom Egypt (up 0.75%). Overall, market breadth was positive, with only one stock posting a decline.
