### English
Today's EGX session was mostly down, with more decliners than gainers. The leading gainer was Oriental Weavers, up 2.04% to EGP 26.49, followed by e‑Finance Investment Group, which rose 0.69% to EGP 24.97. The biggest losers were Talaat Moustafa Group, down 2.04% to EGP 83.95, and Telecom Egypt, down 1.49% to EGP 133.00. Overall, only a handful of stocks posted modest gains while the majority slipped, keeping the index under pressure.
