### English
Today's EGX session was mostly down, with the majority of listed stocks slipping. Telecom Egypt led the gains, up 2.12% to EGP 134.30, while the only other stocks posting a positive move were flat at 0.00% (Abou Kir Fertilizers and Orascom Construction). The biggest decliners were Heliopolis Housing, down 3.25% to EGP 8.03, and Oriental Weavers, down 2.96% to EGP 27.22. Overall, most indices reflected modest losses and market breadth was negative.
