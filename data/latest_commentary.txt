### English
The EGX session was largely bearish, with the majority of stocks posting declines. The only gainers were Commercial International Bank, up 0.42% to EGP 128.74, and Oriental Weavers, up 0.37% to EGP 27.09. The steepest falls were recorded by Elsewedy Electric and Misr Fertilizers Production, each sliding 4.26% to EGP 113.45 and EGP 45.00 respectively. Other notable decliners included Palm Hills Development (‑2.96%), Eastern Company (‑3.66%) and Alexandria Mineral Oils (‑3.73%). Overall, the index reflected pressure on construction and fertilizer sectors, while banking remained the sole bright spot.
