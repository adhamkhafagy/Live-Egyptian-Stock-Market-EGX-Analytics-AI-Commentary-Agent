### English
Today's EGX session was mostly down, with more stocks losing ground than gaining. The biggest riser was Palm Hills Development, up 1.45% to EGP 13.25, followed by e‑Finance Investment Group, which rose 0.96% to EGP 24.22. The sharpest declines came from Abou Kir Fertilizers, down 1.97% to EGP 86.88, and Misr Fertilizers Production, down 1.90% to EGP 46.40. Six stocks posted modest gains, the largest of which was a 0.61% rise in Telecom Egypt to EGP 151.00. Meanwhile, nine stocks closed lower, keeping pressure on the broader index.
