### English
Today's EGX session was mostly down, with the broader market pressured by a cluster of declines. The biggest winner was EFG Holding, which jumped 5.02% to EGP 25.30, followed by Palm Hills Development up 0.52% at EGP 11.50. On the downside, Alexandria Mineral Oils led the losses, slipping 1.78% to EGP 13.26, while Misr Fertilizers Production fell 1.73% to EGP 38.17. Six additional stocks posted losses exceeding 1%, including Elsewedy Electric and Heliopolis Housing. Gains were limited to four stocks and did not offset the overall weakness.
