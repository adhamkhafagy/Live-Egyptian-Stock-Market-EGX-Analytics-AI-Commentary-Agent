The session was largely positive, with most EGX stocks posting gains. Elsewedy Electric led the rally, jumping 4.22% to EGP 120.89, while Telecom Egypt added 3.13% to close at EGP 118.49. On the downside, Alexandria Mineral Oils was the only stock to slip, falling 1.94% to EGP 11.13, and Orascom Construction was flat, unchanged at EGP 71.05. Gains were also seen across sectors, including Oriental Weavers (+2.52%) and Credit Agricole Egypt (+2.12%). Overall, the market breadth was bullish, driven by strong moves in the industrial and telecom segments.
