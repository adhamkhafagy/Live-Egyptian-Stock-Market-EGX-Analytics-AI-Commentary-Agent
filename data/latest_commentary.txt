### English
The EGX session ended mostly lower, with the majority of listed stocks posting declines. Heliopolis Housing led the gains, up 1.53% to EGP 7.95, followed by Misr Fertilizers Production, which rose 1.05% to EGP 47.10. The biggest drags were Oriental Weavers, down 3.84% to EGP 26.57, and Eastern Company, down 3.47% to EGP 33.10. Other notable decliners included Telecom Egypt (‑0.24%) and Elsewedy Electric (‑0.87%). Overall, only two stocks managed positive returns while ten fell, indicating a broadly bearish close.
