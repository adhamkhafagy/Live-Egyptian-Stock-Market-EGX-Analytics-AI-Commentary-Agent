### English
Today's EGX session was mostly down, with more stocks declining than advancing. The biggest gainers were Alexandria Mineral Oils, up 7.08% to EGP 12.25, and Credit Agricole Egypt, up 2.99% to EGP 25.45. The steepest losses came from Oriental Weavers, down 2.24% to EGP 25.76, and e‑Finance Investment Group, down 1.93% to EGP 22.90. Other notable movers included Eastern Company (+2.08%), Abou Kir Fertilizers (+1.89%) and Telecom Egypt, which slipped 0.14% to EGP 116.00. Overall, the index reflected modest volatility with a slight bias to the downside.
