### English
The session was mostly down, with eight of the fifteen listed stocks closing lower. Telecom Egypt led the gains, up 3.94% to EGP 132.00, followed by e‑Finance Investment Group, which rose 2.26% to EGP 23.52. The biggest drags were Palm Hills Development, down 1.36% at EGP 13.81, and Misr Fertilizers Production, down 1.27% to EGP 46.50. Other decliners included Oriental Weavers, Alexandria Mineral Oils and Elsewedy Electric, each losing between 0.30% and 0.40%. Orascom Construction was the only stock to finish unchanged, while several modest gainers such as Credit Agricole Egypt and Heliopolis Housing added less than 1%.
