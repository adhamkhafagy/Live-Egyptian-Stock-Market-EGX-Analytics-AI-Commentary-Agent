### English
The EGX session was mostly down, with more stocks declining than advancing. The biggest gainers were Alexandria Mineral Oils up 4.87% to EGP 14.20 and Abou Kir Fertilizers up 4.53% to EGP 92.00. The steepest declines were seen in Oriental Weavers, which fell 3.20% to EGP 28.10, and Palm Hills Development, down 2.67% to EGP 14.60. Other notable movers included Misr Fertilizers Production gaining 2.17% and Telecom Egypt rising 1.62%, while Elsewedy Electric slipped 1.21% and Credit Agricole Egypt dropped 1.31%. Overall, the index reflected a bearish bias as the majority of listed shares closed lower.
