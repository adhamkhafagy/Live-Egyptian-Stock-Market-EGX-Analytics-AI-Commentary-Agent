### English
Today's EGX session was mostly up, with gains outweighing losses. The biggest advance came from Misr Fertilizers Production, which rose 4.71% to EGP 48.69, followed by Heliopolis Housing up 2.87% at EGP 8.23. On the downside, Eastern Company fell 2.02% to EGP 32.55, and e‑Finance Investment Group slipped 1.06% to EGP 23.27. Overall, most stocks traded within a narrow range, and the index ended the day with modest positive momentum.
