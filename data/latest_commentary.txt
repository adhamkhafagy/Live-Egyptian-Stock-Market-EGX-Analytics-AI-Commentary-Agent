### English
The EGX session was mixed, with a modest bias toward declines as more stocks slipped than rose. Telecom Egypt led the gains, jumping 7.52% to EGP 143.00, while Alexandria Mineral Oils added 2.45% to close at EGP 13.36. On the downside, EFG Holding fell 4.97% to EGP 24.10 and Palm Hills Development dropped 4.44% to EGP 11.85, marking the steepest losses. Other blue‑chip names such as Credit Agricole Egypt (+1.12%) and Elsewedy Electric (+0.99%) posted modest advances, whereas Commercial International Bank was virtually flat at +0.05%.
